## Locked settings (do not touch)

- **Resolution:** every model trains at **224×224** (CIFAR 32 / MNIST 28 are bicubic-upsampled in the data pipeline). Fair-comparison invariant.
- **Convergence-first:** 60 epochs, patience 10, monitor `val_acc`, AdamW + cosine, gradient clip 1.0, label smoothing 0.1 (CNNs and TransNeXt FT alike), drop_path 0.1 (TransNeXt only), batch 32 (fallback 16 + grad-accum 2).
- **Precision:** `bf16-mixed` on Blackwell, `32-true` on CPU. `torch.compile = none` for all models.
- **TransNeXt FT regime:** full FT, head 5e-4 / backbone 5e-5, `pretrain_size=224`, `patch_size=4`.
- **Optuna contract:** 6 winner JSONs at `artifacts/best_hparams/{m}_{d}.json` with `validated_at_full_convergence: true`, `effective_batch_size`, `priors_file_hash`, `n_trials_completed ≥ 18`.
- **Pathology matrix (PRD §6.3):** Healthy / Failed Convergence / Overfitting verdicts with the thresholds defined there. Each cell may be retried **at most once** under §6.4 Full FT; second failure → `QUARANTINED_AFTER_RETRY` sentinel.
- **Git/sync:** tracker writes through [`scripts/refresh_trackers.py`](scripts/refresh_trackers.py); commits through [`scripts/sync_trackers_git.py`](scripts/sync_trackers_git.py) with `FORBIDDEN_FLAGS = {--force, --no-verify, -f, -i}`. **Exactly three** per-phase boundary pushes (A-close, B-close, C-close) — never per-cell.
- **Weight isolation:** `runs/final/**`, `artifacts/optuna_thz.db`, `artifacts/weights/`, all `*.ckpt`/`*.pt`/`*.pth` are gitignored + claudeignored. Never `Read` or `cat` them.
