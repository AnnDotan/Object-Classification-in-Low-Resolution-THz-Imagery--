\section{Final Acceptance Tests}
\label{sec:tests}

This section reports the measured performance of the delivered product
against every relevant metric. Every value comes from a frozen training run
recorded under \texttt{runs/final/} and aggregated by
\texttt{artifacts/Final\_Exp.json} (schema version 3). No values are
synthesized or interpolated.

\subsection{Test rig}
The campaign ran on a single workstation with the configuration in
Table \ref{tab:rig}. Determinism is guarded by
\texttt{pl.seed\_everything(42, workers=True)} for the canonical seed and by
the per-sample seed offset for the validation split.

\subsection{Accuracy versus image quality}
\label{sec:tests:psnr}

Figures \ref{fig:acc_vs_psnr} and \ref{fig:acc_vs_ssim} plot validation
accuracy against the per-pipeline mean PSNR and SSIM respectively. The
Spearman rank correlation is reported in each panel and is monotonically
strong on both metrics for both datasets. Accuracy and image quality
collapse together, but the slope is not unity: on CIFAR-10 the model
loses accuracy faster than SSIM, while on MNIST the two metrics track
each other closely. This corroborates the per-axis attribution in
Section \ref{sec:tests:phase_c}: CIFAR-10 carries more inter-class color
information that is destroyed by the saturation lerp, and SSIM does not
penalize the color collapse strongly.

\begin{figure}[htbp]

\subsection{THz protocol simplification (Phase B2 and B2-nr)}
\label{sec:tests:phase_b2}

Phase B2 simplifies the THz protocol by forcing saturation $=0$ (full
grayscale) and noise $=0$ (no additive noise). T3 regularization is still
applied. Phase B2-nr is the no-regularization arm at L3 only, used to
decompose the Phase B1 to B2 improvement into pure-protocol and
regularization fractions. Table \ref{tab:phase_b2} reports the accuracy
block.

\input{tables/tab_VIII_phase_b2.tex}

CNN backbones inflate confidence as the spatial information collapses,
which is consistent with the reported ECE growth from below 5 \Mpp{} at
L3 to above 15 \Mpp{} at L5 on CIFAR-10 \cite{guo2017oncalibration}.

\subsection{Inference throughput}
\label{sec:tests:throughput}

Figure \ref{fig:throughput} reports the wall-clock inference latency for
each backbone on the test rig. ResNet50 and DenseNet121 fall comfortably
under the 50 ms per image acceptance criterion from the preliminary
specification; TransNeXt-tiny is roughly $1.5\times$ slower because
\texttt{torch.compile} is disabled (the foveal-attention path is not
Inductor-graph-capturable on the 2.11 stack).
