\section{Experimental phases at a glance}
\label{sec:phases}

The campaign decomposes the THz-like robustness question into seven
phases. Each phase opens with the pipeline it uses and closes with the
research question that motivates it.

\textbf{Phase A (Clean baseline).}\quad Pipeline: identity. Six cells
(three models $\times$ two datasets). Establishes the upper-bound
clean accuracy that every later phase is measured against.

\textbf{Phase B1 (Combined degradation).}\quad Pipeline: saturation lerp
$\rightarrow$ downsample $\rightarrow$ upsample to $224 \times 224$
$\rightarrow$ blur $\rightarrow$ additive noise $\rightarrow$
salt-and-pepper, with all five axes active at the same level. Thirty
cells (three models $\times$ two datasets $\times$ five levels).
Quantifies the realistic worst-case combined-degradation curve.

\textbf{Phase C (Single-axis isolation).}\quad Pipeline: exactly one
axis is active at level $L$ while the other four are held at identity.
One hundred fifty cells (three models $\times$ two datasets $\times$
five levels $\times$ five axes). Decomposes the Phase B1 collapse into
per-axis contributions.

\textbf{Phase D (Regularization-recovery sweep).}\quad Pipeline:
identical to Phase B1, plus one of three training-time regularization
treatments T1, T2, T3 (architectural dropout, label mixing, kitchen
sink). Ninety cells (three models $\times$ two datasets $\times$ three
treatments $\times$ five levels). Asks whether the Phase B1 collapse
is an overfit (regularization should recover it) or an information
bottleneck (recovery should be marginal).

\textbf{Phase B2 (THz-protocol simplification).}\quad Pipeline: Phase
B1 minus the saturation and additive-noise axes; T3 regularization
active. Thirty cells. Models the realistic THz acquisition (a
single-channel low-resolution blurry frame with no chroma noise).

\textbf{Phase B2-nr (No-regularization arm).}\quad Same pipeline as
Phase B2, at L3 only, T3 off. Six cells. Decomposes the
Phase B1$\to$B2 accuracy gain into a pure-protocol component and a
regularization component.

\textbf{Phase C2 (Single-axis isolation under the THz protocol).}\quad
Same identity-elsewhere logic as Phase C, restricted to the three
THz-relevant axes (resolution, blur, salt-and-pepper). Ninety cells.
Per-axis attribution under deployment-realistic conditions; the
\S\ref{sec:tests} plots use PSNR and SSIM on the $x$-axis to compare the
axes on a common image-quality scale rather than the predetermined level
index.
