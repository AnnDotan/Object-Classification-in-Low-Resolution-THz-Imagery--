\section*{Appendix B: Supplementary Material --- Data Availability}
\label{app:tables}

The complete per-cell accuracy blocks for every phase (Phase A clean baselines,
Phase B1 combined degradation, the full Phase C single-axis sweep, Phase C2,
the Phase D regularization sweep, Phase B2 / B2-nr, and the multi-seed audit)
are recorded in machine-readable form and are not reproduced here. They are
available in the campaign artifacts: the aggregated
\texttt{artifacts/Final\_Exp.json} (schema version 3) and the per-phase CSV
tables under \texttt{Final\_Report/data/tables/}. The two phases analysed in
detail in Section~\ref{sec:tests} --- Phase B1 and Phase C2 --- are tabulated
inline there (Tables~\ref{tab:phase_b} and~\ref{tab:phase_c2}); the derived
analysis tables (drop versus clean baseline, per-level margins, Spearman
correlations) are regenerated from those CSVs by
\texttt{Final\_Report/V6/scripts/build\_v6\_assets.py}, which also
cross-checks them against the PRD reference numbers at build time.
